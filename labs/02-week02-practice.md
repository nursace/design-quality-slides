# Week 2 Practice — Quality attributes & scenarios (2 h)

**Course:** Analysis and Assessment of Design Quality (MSc SE)  
**Pairs with:** `../sources/02-week02-lecture.tex`

---

## Goals

- Prioritize attributes for a brief  
- Write structured scenarios (source, stimulus, environment, artifact, response, **response measure**)  
- Surface conflicts and non-goals  

---

## Brief (shared)

**Product:** “CampusEats” — students order from campus vendors; courier or pickup.  
**Constraints:** small team (6 engineers); must launch in one term; card payments via external PSP; personal data (student ID, phone).  
**Known pain:** lunch peak 12:00–13:00; vendors change menus daily; refunds disputed.

---

## Part A — Priorities (25 min)

1. Pick **top-3** quality attributes for v1 and justify each in ≤2 sentences.  
2. List **two non-goals** for v1 (explicit).  
3. Note one attribute you *deprioritized* and why.

---

## Part B — Scenarios (50 min)

Write **three** full scenarios (one per top attribute) using all six parts.  
At least one must be a **failure / degraded** scenario.

**Peer challenge (15 min):** swap with a partner. Can they state a pass/fail test without asking you? Mark gaps.

---

## Part C — Conflict matrix (30 min)

1. Build a 3×3 matrix of your top attributes: where do they conflict for CampusEats?  
2. Propose **one cheap mitigation** for the sharpest conflict (checkable in ≤2 weeks of work).  

---

## Done checklist

- [ ] Top-3 + justifications + 2 non-goals  
- [ ] Three full scenarios with response measures  
- [ ] Peer challenge notes  
- [ ] Conflict matrix + one mitigation  
- [ ] Lecturer checkpoint

---

## Stretch

Draft a shallow utility tree: attributes → scenarios → H/M/L importance × risk.
