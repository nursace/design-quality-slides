# Week 7 Practice — Case-study evaluation lab (2 h)

**Course:** Analysis and Assessment of Design Quality (MSc SE)  
**Pairs with:** `../sources/07-week07-lecture.tex`  
**Format:** teams of 3–4

---

## Goals

- Produce a mid-length evaluation memo under time pressure  
- Mark assumptions explicitly  
- Improve evidence clarity via gallery feedback  

---

## Case choice

Lecturer assigns **Messaging** or **Campus registration** (narratives from lecture), or provides a pack.  
If none: use **CampusEats** from Week 2 plus a quick container sketch your team draws in 10 minutes (label assumptions).

---

## Memo template (90 min drafting)

1. **Context & assumptions** (bullets)  
2. **Top-3 attributes + scenarios** (full measures)  
3. **Findings** (≤6), each with severity and evidence  
4. **Optional metrics** (≤3) + caveats  
5. **Verdict** (accept / accept with findings / major rework)  
6. **Top-3 risks & next steps**  
7. **Sensitivity** (load ×10 *or* team ×3 — pick one)  
8. **Responsibility note** (who is harmed if top risk hits)

Target length: **~1.5–2 pages** + figures.

---

## Gallery walk (25 min)

Post memos (wall / shared folder). Each team leaves sticky notes:

- “Evidence clear?”  
- “Severity justified?”  
- “Assumption missing?”

Incorporate at least **two** sticky notes into a revision pass (15 min).

---

## Stretch

Propose one alternative structure and re-score with the Week 6 rubric (only if time).

---

## Done checklist

- [ ] Team memo draft  
- [ ] Gallery feedback collected  
- [ ] ≥2 feedback items incorporated  
- [ ] Lecturer checkpoint
