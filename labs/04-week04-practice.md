# Week 4 Practice — Design review checklists (2 h)

**Course:** Analysis and Assessment of Design Quality (MSc SE)  
**Pairs with:** `../sources/04-week04-lecture.tex`

---

## Goals

- Run a timed structured review  
- Produce severity-tagged actionable comments  
- Complete author response column  

---

## Roles

Pairs (or triad with scribe). Switch roles if time allows.

**Timebox (25 min review):** 3 context · 10 checklist · 7 findings · 5 author responses.
